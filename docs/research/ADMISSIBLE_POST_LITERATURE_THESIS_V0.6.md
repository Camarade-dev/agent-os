## 1. Executive disposition

The post-M2 proposal **survives only after a material narrowing**.

The following framing may remain as product/system shorthand:

> **The model proposes. Admissible authorizes.**

But after the literature review it must **not** be presented as evidence of a
novel architectural primitive. The corresponding top-level feature families for
semantic/task-alignment admission, authorization, runtime policy enforcement,
provenance-aware action checking, formal pre/postcondition verification,
transactional settlement, effective-state projection, recovery, and authority
continuity are densely occupied or partially occupied by prior art.

The research object that remains is therefore not:

> “Can semantic admission help?”

and not:

> “Is model-proposes / middleware-authorizes a new architecture?”

The surviving scientific object is the externally reclosed empirical question:

> **Does a precisely specified semantic-admission treatment provide incremental value over strong contemporary semantic/task-alignment and structural runtime controls—under a matched model/task/tool/effect substrate, with explicit safety–utility/intervention costs and physically justified scored→executed/effect identity?**

That question may return **NO**.

## 2. Disposition of the frozen V0.3 proposal

### 2.1 RETAIN — product/system framing, not novelty

Retain:

> **The model decides what could be done. Admissible decides what may be done.**

Interpretation after literature:

- a useful conceptual separation between proposal and permission may remain;
- it is a system/product framing;
- it is not a claim that authorization, semantic action admission, or runtime
  governance is architecturally novel.

### 2.2 WITHDRAW — “semantic action admission is the distinctive thesis”

The V0.3 proposal described Layer A semantic admission as the “distinctive
thesis to benchmark.”

That wording is too strong after the literature review.

The corresponding top-level semantic/task/action-alignment and admission feature families are
densely occupied or partially occupied by prior art, including strong peer-reviewed and
pre-publication accepted systems. No novelty inference is allowed.

Post-literature status:

`SEMANTIC_ADMISSION_AS_DISTINCTIVE_ARCHITECTURAL_PRIMITIVE = WITHDRAWN`

What remains is a **candidate treatment** whose incremental value must be isolated
and measured against serious contemporary alternatives.

### 2.3 NARROW — H-M2-CONTINUITY

The V0.3 proposal framed H-M2-CONTINUITY as a narrow benchmark-prerequisite
hypothesis.

The literature further narrows it.

Residual M2 continuity/effect machinery is now:

`CONDITIONAL_CANDIDATE_EXPERIMENTAL_APPARATUS`

not a presumed prerequisite.

Residual M2 continuity/effect machinery is retained only when it materially changes
attribution/classification beyond applicable simpler stages and conditional comparators,
or is required by a selected long-running validity claim; otherwise it should be
minimized or rejected.

The current minimization structure is:

`Vera-like → ToolGate-like → RefineAct-like → residual M2`

with conditional residual-runtime pressure from:

- Atomix-like transactional effect settlement when settlement semantics apply;
- Mnemosyne-like committed-transition/effective-state/repair when
  replay/continuation/lifecycle/recovery/effective-state semantics apply;
- the existing falsification pressures for abnormal tool behavior, procedural
  trace compliance, trusted-looking data/metadata injection, and related cases.

The difficulty and sunk cost of M2 provide zero evidence of necessity.

### 2.4 RETAIN — physical honesty as a validity requirement

One V0.3 idea survives in a weaker and more defensible form:

> A benchmark must not score a semantic admission decision using an effect,
> state, or proof relation that the experiment cannot physically justify.

This is not Admissible novelty.

It is an experimental-validity requirement.

The implementation used to achieve it should be minimized and shared between
comparison arms wherever possible.

### 2.5 WITHDRAW — broad benchmark contrast

A comparison of:

`raw/frontier agent` versus `agent + Admissible`

is not a scientifically sufficient primary comparison.

A future benchmark must represent or justify exclusions for strong comparator
subfamilies, including semantic/task-action alignment, structural/policy or
capability controls, contract-grounded verification, and applicable runtime
settlement/effective-state machinery.

### 2.6 RETAIN AS HYPOTHESIS — incremental semantic-admission value

The following remains an open empirical hypothesis:

> A precisely specified semantic-admission treatment may provide incremental
> safety/authority/evidence value over strong contemporary controls, without
> unacceptable loss of useful safe throughput or excessive intervention cost.

This is **not adopted as fact**.

A negative result against strong baselines is a valid scientific result.

## 4. Industrial thesis remains separate

The industrial program may still pursue:

> a long-running agent system whose proposal, authority, effect, evidence,
> continuation, repair, and owner-intervention semantics remain coherent under
> real production complexity.

The literature review does **not** show that this industrial integration problem
is solved end-to-end.

But it also does not make that integration a novel research primitive.

Therefore:

`INDUSTRIAL_SYSTEM_VALUE != RESEARCH_NOVELTY`

The industrial program may be valuable even if the incremental semantic-admission
research hypothesis returns NO.

## 5. Claims explicitly surrendered

The post-literature position surrenders:

1. semantic admission / model-proposes-authorizer-decides as architectural novelty;
2. broad “does semantic admission help?” first-demonstration framing;
3. a novelty inference from requiring one paper to contain every desired feature;
4. M2 as a novel security/runtime primitive;
5. M2 as a presumed minimal benchmark substrate;
6. effect settlement, recovery, replay, continuation, or effective-state truth
   as unoccupied mechanism space;
7. raw-agent-versus-Admissible as a sufficient serious benchmark;
8. literature absence or search failure as novelty evidence.

## 6. What remains unresolved

The owner-ready literature handoff does **not** answer:

- what the precise Admissible treatment is after subtracting already-known
  comparator mechanisms;
- whether that treatment is causally isolatable on a common substrate;
- whether it has positive incremental value;
- which residual M2 mechanisms are actually necessary;
- which real long-running task should instantiate the system;
- whether provider transport, continuation, evaluator, installed path, owner
  authority, or other gaps are logically next;
- whether the research program should survive a negative treatment-isolation result.
